**Name**: AgriPedia: Garden & Crops

**Short description**: What to plant, when and how: calendar, guides and tips for your region.

**Full description**:

AgriPedia is a farming encyclopedia that tells you which crops suit your region, when to sow or plant them, and how to grow them well. For balconies, vegetable gardens, small and large farms.

🌱 CROPS SUITED TO YOUR REGION
• Choose your country and region, or use your location (optional)
• Suggested crops for the month or season, including the rainy season in tropical climates
• Calendar computed from the local climate: frost, heat, rain, greenhouse or container
• Always shown as indicative, with its assumptions and limits

📖 COMPLETE PLANT SHEETS
• Names in English, Arabic and French, scientific name and family
• Temperatures, soil, pH, water needs, spacing, sowing depth, cycle length
• Varieties, good and bad companions, crop rotation
• Pests and diseases, prevention and control methods
• Step-by-step guide, from soil preparation to harvest and storage
• Every piece of information shows its source and verification level

🔎 SMART SEARCH
• Tolerates typos, accents and Arabic spelling variants
• Filters: category, difficulty, water, soil, climate, growing method, cycle length

📅 YOUR CALENDAR AND CROPS
• Gardens, plots and crops, with crop rotation warnings
• Milestones planned from your own dates: transplanting, checks, harvest
• Tasks, reminders, repeated watering, notes and favorites
• Backup and restore of your data

🌍 FOR EVERYONE
• English, Arabic and French, dark mode
• No account, works offline: your data stays on your phone

The current plant sheets are a first base, to be checked with your local agricultural services; they will be expanded and validated over future versions. The app is free, with a small ad banner. You can support it with a donation.
